## 4. Stakeholder Analysis

Stakeholders are individuals or groups that have an interest in or are impacted by the Sprint Retrospective Analyzer system. They are categorized as primary and secondary stakeholders based on their level of interaction and influence.

### Primary Stakeholders

- **Scrum Masters**  
  Primary users responsible for conducting retrospectives and using insights from the system to drive continuous improvement.

- **Agile Coaches**  
  Use long-term retrospective data to identify recurring patterns, coach teams, and improve Agile maturity.

- **Developers**  
  Provide retrospective feedback and benefit from reduced recurring issues and improved team processes.

- **QA Engineers**  
  Contribute quality-related feedback and use insights to improve testing and release stability.

- **Product Owners**  
  Review improvement trends to understand process bottlenecks and ensure smoother delivery cycles.

### Secondary Stakeholders

- **Organization Management**  
  Interested in high-level process improvement metrics and overall team effectiveness.

- **Project Managers**  
  Use retrospective insights to support planning and risk mitigation, though not directly involved in retrospectives.

- **Academic Evaluators / Faculty**  
  Evaluate the system for learning outcomes, documentation quality, and adherence to software engineering principles.

- **Student Agile Teams**  
  Use the system for educational purposes to learn Agile retrospectives and continuous improvement practices.

This stakeholder analysis helps ensure that the system requirements and design decisions align with the needs and expectations of all relevant parties.
