## 1. Project Selection & Team Formation

The project **Sprint Retrospective Analyzer** was selected to address a significant gap in Agile software development practices. Although sprint retrospectives are conducted regularly, the feedback collected is often unstructured, qualitative, and not analyzed across sprints. This results in recurring issues, lack of accountability for action items, and minimal measurable improvement.  
The chosen project aims to convert sprint retrospectives into a structured, data-driven learning system that supports continuous improvement and aligns strongly with Agile principles.

The project was selected due to its real-world relevance, alignment with Agile development concepts, and suitability for applying software engineering practices such as requirements analysis, system design, and iterative development.
