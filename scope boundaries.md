## 9. Scope Boundaries Document


The scope boundaries document clearly defines the limits of the Sprint Retrospective Analyzer system to avoid ambiguity and scope creep during development. It establishes what the system is responsible for and what is intentionally excluded.


### Within Scope
- Collection of sprint retrospective feedback from Agile team members
- Structuring of feedback into predefined retrospective categories
- Storage of retrospective data across multiple sprints
- Analysis of historical retrospective feedback to identify recurring issues
- Tracking of action items and their outcomes across sprints
- Visualization of trends and insights using dashboards and reports
- Support for both industry-oriented Agile teams and academic users


### Outside Scope
- Sprint planning, task assignment, and backlog management
- Real-time collaboration during retrospective meetings
- Integration with external Agile tools such as Jira or Azure DevOps
- Advanced AI-driven predictive analytics
- Production-scale deployment and enterprise security compliance


Clearly defining these boundaries ensures focused development and realistic project outcomes.
