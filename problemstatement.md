## 3. Problem Statement Development

### Project Title
**Sprint Retrospective Analyzer – A Data-Driven System for Continuous Agile Improvement**

### Problem Definition
In Agile software development, sprint retrospectives are conducted at the end of every sprint to reflect on what went well, what did not go well, and what can be improved. However, the feedback collected during these retrospectives is typically unstructured, qualitative, and documented in informal formats such as notes, chat messages, or whiteboards. Over time, this feedback is rarely revisited or analyzed systematically.

As a result, recurring issues remain undetected, action items lack accountability, and teams are unable to measure whether process improvements are actually effective. Retrospectives often become isolated discussions rather than a continuous learning mechanism.

### Proposed Solution
The **Sprint Retrospective Analyzer** is a web-based system that captures sprint retrospective feedback in a structured manner while preserving free-text input. The system analyzes retrospective data across multiple sprints to identify recurring themes, track trends, and evaluate the effectiveness of action items. By providing analytical insights and visual dashboards, the system enables teams to make data-driven improvements to their Agile processes.

### Target Users
- Scrum Masters  
- Agile Coaches  
- Developers  
- QA Engineers  
- Product Owners  
- Student Agile Teams  

### Value Proposition
The Sprint Retrospective Analyzer transforms retrospectives from subjective, one-time discussions into a measurable and continuous improvement process. It helps Agile teams identify recurring problems, track progress over time, ensure accountability for action items, and make informed decisions based on evidence rather than assumptions.
