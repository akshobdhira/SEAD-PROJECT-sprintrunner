## 2. Team Agreement Creation

The team agreement defines the working norms, responsibilities, and collaboration practices to ensure smooth execution of the **Sprint Retrospective Analyzer** project. The agreement establishes a shared understanding among all team members and promotes accountability, transparency, and effective teamwork throughout the project lifecycle.

### Team Working Norms

- All team members will actively participate in planning, development, testing, and documentation activities.
- Responsibilities assigned to each role will be respected and fulfilled within agreed timelines.
- Team members will communicate regularly through scheduled meetings and online collaboration tools.
- Major technical and design decisions will be discussed collectively before implementation.
- Conflicts or disagreements will be resolved through open discussion and mutual consensus.

### Communication Guidelines

- Weekly team meetings will be conducted to review progress and plan upcoming tasks.
- Urgent issues will be communicated immediately through messaging platforms.
- Project-related updates and decisions will be documented and shared with all members.

### Task Management and Version Control

- All project artifacts, source code, and documentation will be maintained using a shared GitHub repository.
- Version control practices such as meaningful commit messages and branch usage will be followed.
- No changes will be merged into the main branch without prior review by the team.

### Quality and Accountability

- The Lead Developer ensures implementation quality and adherence to requirements.
- The QA Lead is responsible for testing, defect reporting, and validation.
- The Documentation Lead ensures accuracy and consistency of all project documents.
- The Project Manager monitors progress, resolves blockers, and ensures milestone completion.

This team agreement ensures disciplined collaboration, maintains project quality, and supports successful completion of the Sprint Retrospective Analyzer project.
