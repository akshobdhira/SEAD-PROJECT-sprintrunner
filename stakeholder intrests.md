## 5. Stakeholder Interests Documentation

This section documents the interests, expectations, and level of influence of each stakeholder group associated with the Sprint Retrospective Analyzer project. Understanding stakeholder interests helps ensure that system requirements and design decisions align with their needs.

| Stakeholder | Interests / Expectations | Level of Influence |
|------------|--------------------------|-------------------|
| **Scrum Masters** | Effective retrospectives, visibility into recurring issues, measurable improvement across sprints | High |
| **Agile Coaches** | Long-term trend analysis, identification of systemic process issues, data-driven coaching insights | High |
| **Developers** | Reduced repetition of problems, improved team communication, actionable feedback | Medium |
| **QA Engineers** | Better visibility into quality-related issues, fewer recurring defects, improved release stability | Medium |
| **Product Owners** | Process transparency, improved sprint outcomes, alignment between delivery and improvement actions | Medium |
| **Organization Management** | Evidence-based process improvement, team performance insights, operational efficiency | Medium |
| **Project Managers** | Risk reduction, improved planning insights, smoother sprint execution | Low |
| **Academic Evaluators / Faculty** | Correct application of software engineering principles, quality documentation, learning outcomes | High |
| **Student Agile Teams** | Practical understanding of retrospectives and continuous improvement practices | Low |

This documentation ensures that stakeholder priorities are considered appropriately during requirement definition, system design, and evaluation.
